# Bluff v2: Polly's Interrogation (2026-10-31)

Dixon's ask: "pick a game we haven't given a version 2 yet and lets dive in." Every game got a rebuild on Oct 14-15, so the pick was the thinnest one: Bluff and Fake Word shared one engine and one set of screens, two paint jobs on the same game, and Bluff's purple game show was the most generic look on the shelf. Fake Word keeps the shared screens and plays exactly as before.

## What changed

1. **Its own world.** Polly (the polygraph host) runs an interrogation room: slate walls, one swinging lamp, a one-way mirror, manila case files with folder tabs and paper clips, evidence tags for the answers, rubber stamps (LIE, HOUSE LIE, TRUE, NO COMMENT, WINS). Stencil display (Black Ops One) and typewriter text (Special Elite). The phone reskin follows through `.lie-bluff` in `_lie/lie.css`.
2. **The live polygraph.** A chart strip runs along the bottom of the TV with one trace per player in their color: a tremor while they write or pick, flat once they lock in, a jump when they fall for a lie, and off the chart for the author while their lie is being exposed. Drawn from a ring buffer in a rAF loop (no React re-render), `heat()` in `show/Stage.tsx` decides how hard each trace moves.
3. **Category pick.** Before each fact, one player (rotating by seat) picks from three of the bank's categories; 12 s, then Polly picks. Pool entries are `id|cat`, freshest first, never repeating a fact in a game.
4. **About Us.** The closing run: one round per player (up to 4, last one triple, the others double). The question is about that player ("{name}'s first concert ever was ____."). They type the truth (the button says Swear to it), everyone else lies about them, everyone but them picks. Points: finders +1000 x factor, liars +500 per fooled, and the subject +500 x factor for every friend who found their truth. Subject silent at the buzzer: the round is skipped ("pleaded the fifth"). Bank: `bluff/about.ts` (45 questions, three generic House lies each).
5. **Polly's Verdict.** The end screen names the winner and hands out Best liar (most fooled), Easiest mark (most lies fallen for, new `fell` stat) and Truth seeker.

Lengths with About Us on: Short = 3 facts + About Us, Normal = 5 facts + About Us.

## Where it lives

- Engine (shared, extras off unless configured): `src/games/_lie/logic.ts` (`cfg.cats`, `cfg.about`, stage `category`, `pickersFor`, `isAbout`, `TOLD_POINTS`). Tests: `_lie/about.test.ts`; `_lie/bot.test.ts` knows About Us rounds.
- Bluff: `src/games/bluff/index.ts` (turns both on), `about.ts`, `show/Stage.tsx` (TV + compact), `show/lines.ts`, `show/bluff.css`.
- Phone: still the shared `_lie/Controller.tsx`, with category buttons, the subject's truth entry and wait screen, and skipped rounds.
- QA: `scripts/drivers/bluff.mjs` taps the category and the Swear to it button.
