# Turnpike: Kart, rebuilt as a real kart racer (2026-10-14)

Dixon's ask, right after Party Park shipped: "now i want the same build out for the mario kart version. go!" The Party Park ask it points at: "much bigger more mario party like vibe. full build out. go big. in design and UI and UX... music in the background would be nice. choosing characters etc etc skies the limit." Read `docs/plans/2026-10-14-party-park.md` too: same bar, same rules.

Today Kart is a flat top-down canvas: one dark rectangle with an oval, letter-dot karts, four items, a riso phone pad. It works (sim, items, bots, drift, 10 Hz input, tests). **This rebuild keeps the sim core and makes it a kart racer you'd show people**: critters in karts, a chase camera behind each racer, split screen on the TV, Grand Prix cups, eight Florida tracks, Florida items, a soundtrack, a phone that feels like a wheel.

Game id stays `kart` (saved results use it). The shelf name becomes **Turnpike**, `instead: 'Instead of Mario Kart'`.

## The world

Same universe as Party Park. Mingo the flamingo now runs the **Turnpike**, Party Park's go-kart attraction that got permits for the whole state. The eight critters from `src/games/board/park/cast.tsx` are the drivers (CritterId, colors, bios; reuse `CritterArt`, `Mingo`, `Portrait`, `Sticker`, `PartyButton`, `Banner`, `Outlined`, `Coin` from `park/ui.tsx` and `park/cast.tsx`; do not edit those two files, tell the lead if the kit needs a change). Bright daytime sticker look on every screen of this game (night track excepted), not the app's dark riso.

Original everything. "Like Mario Kart" means the shape (character select, cups, chase cam, split screen, item boxes, drift sparks, rocket start, final lap, podium), never its names, characters, sounds or art.

**Contract:** `src/games/kart/contract.ts` (stages, timings, cups, tracks, items, scene shape, streams). Only the lead edits it. If you need a change, say so in your report and work around it with a local type meanwhile.

## The game

- **Options** (index.ts): Race = `Grand Prix (4 races)` | `Single race`; Cup = Sunshine | Hurricane; Track (single race only, all eight); Speed = Sunday Driver | Rush Hour | Florida Man (default Rush Hour); Gas = Always on | Hold to go; TV camera = Split screen | Overview (default split; overview for 5+ racers automatically). Apart = Race | Time trial (default Race).
- **Flow:** `pick` (every phone picks a critter, unique, 45 s, auto-fill; bots auto-pick) → `cupIntro` (cup card, the four tracks) → per race: `trackIntro` (flyover of the track with its name card) → `countdown` (light tree 3-2-1-GO, rocket start window) → `race` → `freeze` (finish line held 3 s after everyone finishes or the window closes) → `raceResult` (places, cup points counting up, standings re-sorting) → next race → `podium` (top three critters in karts on the podium, cup trophy, confetti) → `done`. Single race skips cupIntro and goes raceResult → podium.
- **Outcome:** Grand Prix ranks by cup points (ties by best last-race place); single race by place. Highlights: most hits, best comeback, fastest lap, rocket starts.
- **Apart mode** is a real race now: the host phone runs the sim; every phone streams input and draws its own chase cam from `kart:pos` (interpolated). Time trial stays as the Apart option.

## The driving (sim)

Keep `sim.ts` deterministic and tested; extend it.
- Mini-turbo: drift charges blue → orange → pink sparks (tiers 1-3, `mt`), release = boost of rising length.
- Rocket start: gas pressed inside the last `ROCKET_WINDOW_MS` before GO = boost; pressed too early = a sputter.
- Slipstream: right behind someone for ~1.2 s = a small boost.
- Coins on the road (max 10, each a little top speed; a hit drops 3, scattered).
- Boost pads, jump ramps (`z`, airtime, a trick if hop is tapped in the air = small boost on landing), off-road (grass, sand, mud slows unless boosting).
- Items (`TurnpikeItem`): Cafecito, Triple Cafecito, Mango (peel), Coconut (straight, bounces twice), Pelican (homes on the kart ahead), Floatie (shield), Love Bugs (splats the view of everyone ahead 3 s), Storm (everyone else spins and drives slow 3 s; rare, back of the pack only), Hurricane (hits the leader and anyone near; rare, back only). Item box roulette `ROULETTE_MS`, distribution by place (leaders get Mango/Coconut/Floatie, the back gets the big ones).
- Hazards: Swamp gator (exists), Keys wind gusts (lateral push, telegraphed), Launch Pad rocket liftoff on lap 2 (shake, a smoke cloud slows a section), I-4 construction barrels that move lanes each lap, Pier waves that splash the end of the pier.
- Bots: rubber-banded by speed class, use items with intent (Mango behind when someone is close, Coconut when someone is ahead in line, Floatie held while leading), drift and mini-turbo, take boost pads. Competent, beatable, not psychic.

## Tracks (8)

Sunshine Cup: **Gate Loop** (`oval`, the go-kart ring at the Party Park front gate, a Mingo statue in the infield), **Flyover** (`eight`, a figure eight through a highway interchange, over and under), **Gator Swamp** (`swamp`, airboats, cypress, the gator), **I-4 at Night** (`night`, downtown neon, cones and barrels, "construction until 2047").
Hurricane Cup: **Pier Pressure** (`pier`, boardwalk then a long pier over the Gulf and back), **Coaster Hill** (`coaster`, through Party Park under the coaster loop, a jump over the lazy river), **Seven Mile Bridge** (`keys`, mostly bridge over open water, wind), **Launch Pad** (`launch`, Space Coast, the rocket goes up mid-race).
Each track: geometry (the world is 1600x900 today; bigger worlds are fine now that the camera follows, keep a sane size), `TrackFeatures`, `TrackLook`, `Prop[]` (dense enough that the chase cam feels like a place: 40-120 props), a blurb in the house voice. Lap time on Rush Hour: 25-40 s.

## The look and feel

- **TV, split screen:** one chase-cam pane per racer (1 = full, 2 = side by side, 3 = 2 on top + 1 wide below or 3 columns with a minimap in the fourth quadrant, 4 = quad), each pane with its own HUD (place huge in the corner, lap, item box, coins). A shared minimap. 5-6 racers = Overview (tilted 3D track, whole course, the leaders framed).
- **Chase cam:** a Mode 7 style ground plane (track painted once to a big offscreen canvas, drawn per scanline with perspective), a sky gradient + horizon band, and billboard sprites sorted by depth (props, item boxes, coins, karts, items in flight). Camera sits behind and above the kart, lags a touch on turns, pulls back slightly at speed, FOV kick on boosts, shake on hits. Drift sparks, boost flames, dust off-road, splash on water, shadows under jumping karts.
- **Karts:** each critter in its own kart, sprites drawn for 8 view angles (from behind, three-quarter, side, front) and poses (straight, lean left, lean right, spin, trick, hurt). Kart body in the critter's color.
- **Type:** Lilita One (`font-party`) for display and numbers, Atkinson for longer copy. TV body 24px+, titles 48px+, place numbers huge.
- **Motion:** squash and stretch, pops with overshoot, one focal motion per moment. CSS transforms on DOM; canvas for the race. 60 fps on a laptop for 3 panes.
- **No emojis anywhere. No em dashes in copy.** Mingo's voice: theme park employee on hour eleven of a double ("Keep all limbs inside the kart. Or don't. I'm not your mom.").

## Sound

Synthesized like Party Park (`park/music.ts`): a theme per track (eight, each its own groove: steel drum surf for Pier, airboat banjo swamp, synthwave I-4, space coast arpeggios...), title (pick, cupIntro), results, podium, plus a final-lap version (same theme, faster, up a step). Stingers: countdown beeps (3, 2, 1, GO as one stinger timed to `COUNTDOWN_MS`), lap, finalLap, finish, win, lose, item get, item hit, boost, coin, rocket start, storm, hurricane. The existing music switch and `gn-music-off` key apply.

## Rules of the road

- Read the repo `CLAUDE.md` first. Logic and sim stay pure and deterministic. Never mutate state.
- Work only inside your worktree (absolute paths). Commit on your branch with explicit paths (never `git add -A`). Do not push, do not merge, do not touch other lanes' files.
- **No unseen UI:** render every screen you change (TV 1280x720, phone 390x844 and landscape 844x390), read your own screenshots, fix what looks wrong, re-shoot. Judge animation by its worst frame.
- Report: what you built, file list, screenshot paths that prove it, perf numbers where relevant, anything unverified, anything you need from another lane.
